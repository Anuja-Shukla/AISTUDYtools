The loop

How it works in class

  1. CreateTurn concepts, cases and classroom material into realistic practice scenarios.
  2. EngageStudents apply knowledge, make decisions and solve problems, not just memorise.
  3. AssessEvaluate responses and spot strengths, gaps and areas to improve.
  4. GuideUse the feedback to help students improve and build practical skills.
