Use cases

Use cases for faculty

Common teaching problems, the tool that solves each one, and what you will have at the end.

Faculty

Turn a lecture into a role-play

The challenge: Students read about negotiation but rarely practise it.

Use: Simulation Creator

What you get: A ready-to-paste role-play prompt with a client persona and debrief questions, in about ten minutes.

Faculty

Discuss this week's news as a case

The challenge: Textbook cases feel dated and students disengage.

Use: Quick Case Generator

What you get: A short, classroom-ready case from a current news story, plus a separate teaching note.

Faculty

Revise a chapter with a quiz game

The challenge: Revision classes turn into a lecture repeat.

Use: Interactive Game Creator

What you get: Drag-and-drop quiz games from your lesson, shared with the class as one link.

Faculty

Mark answer scripts more consistently

The challenge: Feedback on 60 short answers takes hours and varies from script to script.

Use: AI evaluator

What you get: A rubric score, key-point check and feedback draft for each answer. You review and decide every grade.

Faculty

Choose a journal for a paper

The challenge: Checking ABDC, Scopus quartile and Web of Science status for each journal is slow.

Use: Journal Finder

What you get: All three ratings in one search, filterable by field.

Faculty

Make slides from your notes

The challenge: Building a deck from scratch eats preparation time.

Use: AI Presentation Creator

What you get: A designed PowerPoint with charts, ready to edit.
