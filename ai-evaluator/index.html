Good to know

How it works, and its limits

Rubric first

Choose a preset or write your own criteria and weights. The tool suggests a level from 1 to 4 for each criterion.

Key points and evidence

It checks which expected points appear, counts examples, figures and sources, and looks for reasoning words.

Claims to verify

Sentences with figures or absolute words but no source are flagged so you can check them.

Private and in your hands

Everything runs in your browser. The tool cannot judge truth or detect AI authorship; your judgement decides the grade.
