Use cases

For faculty

Faculty

Turn a lecture into a role-play

The challenge: Students read about negotiation but rarely practise it.

Use: Simulation Creator

What you get: A ready-to-paste role-play prompt with a client persona and debrief questions, in about ten minutes.

Faculty

Discuss this week's news as a case

The challenge: Textbook cases feel dated and students disengage.

Use: Quick Case Generator

What you get: A short, classroom-ready case from a current news story, plus a separate teaching note.

Faculty

Revise a chapter with a quiz game

The challenge: Revision classes turn into a lecture repeat.

Use: Interactive Game Creator

What you get: Drag-and-drop quiz games from your lesson, shared with the class as one link.

Faculty

Mark answer scripts more consistently

The challenge: Feedback on 60 short answers takes hours and varies from script to script.

Use: AI evaluator

What you get: A rubric score, key-point check and feedback draft for each answer. You review and decide every grade.

Faculty

Choose a journal for a paper

The challenge: Checking ABDC, Scopus quartile and Web of Science status for each journal is slow.

Use: Journal Finder

What you get: All three ratings in one search, filterable by field.

Faculty

Make slides from your notes

The challenge: Building a deck from scratch eats preparation time.

Use: AI Presentation Creator

What you get: A designed PowerPoint with charts, ready to edit.

For students

Students

Check a draft before submitting

The challenge: An AI-assisted draft may miss key points or contain unsupported claims.

Use: AI evaluator (AI-output preset)

What you get: A list of missing points and claims that need a source. Always follow your institution's AI-use policy.

Students

Learn to prompt before an internship

The challenge: Generic prompts give generic, unreliable answers.

Use: Prompt Engineering course

What you get: A free certificate and reusable prompt templates for study and work.

Students

Prepare for a statistics test

The challenge: Formulas are easy to forget without practice.

Use: Basics of Statistics course

What you get: Calculators that show every step, quick checks and a certificate.

Students

Practise decisions, not just theory

The challenge: Exams test recall; jobs need judgement.

Use: Student Corner simulations and games

What you get: Branching simulations with instant feedback, and games for quick revision.

For professionals

Professionals

Get found in AI search

The challenge: Customers now ask ChatGPT and Google's AI answers instead of browsing.

Use: SEO to GEO course

What you get: A checklist for answer-first, citable content, robots.txt rules and a way to track AI visibility.

Professionals

Automate routine enquiries

The challenge: Staff answer the same admissions or HR questions every day.

Use: Building AI Agents course

What you get: An agent brief, instructions and a tested flow with human checkpoints, built without code.

Professionals

Plan hiring with data

The challenge: Hiring plans are guesses that ignore attrition.

Use: HR Analytics course

What you get: Workforce plans, HR metrics and a Power BI dashboard approach you can reuse.

Professionals

Prioritise a product backlog

The challenge: Every stakeholder says their feature is urgent.

Use: Product Management course

What you get: RICE scoring, market sizing and A/B test checks with interactive calculators.
