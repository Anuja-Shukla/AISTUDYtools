AIgametools

Turn any lesson into a classroom game. Upload a PDF or your own MCQs, share a code, and students play Snakes & Ladders, Ludo or Jump Quest. They answer questions to earn coins, and a live leaderboard tracks the class.

Three games, one rule: answer to earn

🐍

Snakes & Ladders

Each roll costs coins. Land on a snake and answer a rescue question to escape it. Beat the bots to 100.

🎯

Ludo

Race your four tokens home against 1 or 3 bots. When a bot is about to capture you, answer correctly to defend your token.

🤖

Jump Quest

Run and jump with Jugg the robot. Collect coins, open quiz crates for bonus questions, and answer to save a life when you fall.

How it works

  1. Add questions. Upload a PDF and questions are made automatically, paste or upload your own MCQs, or pick a ready-made set.
  2. Share the code. Students join on their phones by scanning the QR code or typing the code. No sign-up and no app.
  3. Play and learn. Everyone starts with coins. Game moves cost coins and mistakes lose them, so students answer questions to earn more. Missed questions come back later.
  4. See who learned what. A live leaderboard during the game, then a report showing the hardest questions, which you can download as a CSV file.

Built for classrooms

Rescue questions

Learning is built into the danger moments: dodge a snake, save a token, or get a second life by answering correctly.

Streak bonus

Correct answers in a row earn extra coins, up to +10 per question.

Smart repeat

A question a student gets wrong comes back a few questions later until they get it right.

Private by design

No accounts. Questions and scores pass directly between the teacher's browser and students' phones and are not stored on our servers.
