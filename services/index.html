2 · AI evaluator

Grade fairly. Give feedback that teaches.

Score student answers or AI-generated text against your rubric, in seconds. You stay in charge of every grade.

  • 1Your rubric: presets for essays, cases and AI output, or your own criteria and weights.
  • 2Key-point check: see which expected points are covered and which are missing.
  • 3Claims to verify: figures without a source and absolute claims are flagged.
  • 4Weighted score that updates when you change any level.
  • 5Feedback in one click, ready to share with the student.
