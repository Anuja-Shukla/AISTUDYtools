FAQ

Questions

Are the courses free?

Seven courses are completely free, with a certificate. CA Foundation, CSEET and SSC CGL are paid (from ₹1,999 a year), and you can try the first 2 lessons of each paper free.

How do I get a certificate?

Finish the lessons and score at least 70% in the final test. Your certificate is ready to download straight away, and it stays in My profile.

Do I need to create an account?

Just register once with your name and email. It takes 20 seconds, and you can see your details and certificates any time in My profile.

Do the faculty tools need an API key?

Some do. The Simulation Creator and AI evaluator work without one; others use your own free Google Gemini key, which stays on your device.

Can my institution use AIStudyTools?

Yes. We set up courses, tools and AI-assisted evaluation for departments and whole institutions. See For institutions or talk to us.
