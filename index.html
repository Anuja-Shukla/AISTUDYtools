The science

Why gamification works

Games are not just fun. When game elements serve the learning goal, students learn more, stay motivated and keep coming back.

Learning ↑Better knowledge and understanding
effect size g = 0.49
Motivation ↑More interest and engagement
effect size g = 0.36
Participation ↑More active learning behaviour
effect size g = 0.25

Source: Sailer & Homner (2020), The gamification of learning: a meta-analysis, Educational Psychology Review. Positive effects were found on all three types of outcome. Read the study

Instant feedback

Students know straight away what they got right and what to fix, while the idea is still fresh.

Recall, not re-reading

Quizzes and challenges make students pull ideas from memory, which strengthens long-term learning.

Visible progress

Points, levels and progress bars turn a long syllabus into small, achievable goals.

Story and teamwork

A game story and team challenges that mix competition with collaboration keep learners involved.
