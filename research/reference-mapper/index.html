Good to know

  • Works best on papers with a clear “References”, “Bibliography” or “Works Cited” heading.
  • Scanned PDFs have no text to read: upload the Word file or paste the text instead.
  • Titles are kept exactly as written. APA asks for sentence case in titles, so check capitals before you submit.
  • Always glance over the result. Automatic reading is fast but not perfect; use Edit for any entry.
