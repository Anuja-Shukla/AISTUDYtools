Before you submit

What the Reference Mapper checks

Every citation has a reference

Citations in the text with no entry in the reference list are listed, so nothing goes missing.

Every reference is cited

Entries in the list that are never cited are flagged for you to cite or remove.

Years and names agree

Mismatched years (Smith, 2019 in the text, 2018 in the list) and possible duplicates are shown.

Style is consistent

Authors, italics, volume, issue, pages and DOIs are rewritten the same way for every entry.
