Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Refunds

If you are not satisfied with a paid course, email intelliqueresearchschool@gmail.com within 7 days of purchase with your payment ID, and we will give you a full refund, provided you have not yet received the course certificate.

Approved refunds are made to the original payment method through Razorpay, usually within 5–7 working days. Your access to the course ends when the refund is issued.

Duplicate or failed payments

If you were charged twice, or money was deducted but you did not get access, contact us with the payment ID and we will either give you access or refund the extra amount in full.

Cancellation

Courses are digital and delivered immediately, so there is nothing to cancel after purchase; use the refund option above. If we cancel or withdraw a course you paid for before you can access it, we refund you in full.

Delivery

Paid courses are delivered online on this website. Access starts right after a successful payment (or on the launch date shown on the course page). Nothing is shipped.
