# SSC CGL Reasoning — Analogy
Welcome! This SSC CGL lesson is about analogy, the first reasoning topic. || Welcome! This S S C C G L lesson is about analogy, the first topic in general intelligence and reasoning.
A : B :: C : ? Find the rule in the first pair, apply it to the second. || An analogy reads A is to B as C is to what. Find the rule that links the first pair, and apply the same rule to the second.
Make a sentence: a pen is used to write; a knife is used to cut. || For word pairs, put the relationship into a short sentence. A pen is used to write. Now test the options: a knife is used to cut. Common relationships are tool and use, worker and workplace, animal and young one, and part and whole.
Number pairs: try squares, cubes, n² ± 1 and n(n + 1). || For numbers, look for a simple rule. Three to twenty seven is a cube. Five to twenty six is n squared plus one. Seven to fifty six is n times n plus one.
Letters: learn the positions. EJOTY = 5, 10, 15, 20, 25. || For letters, learn the alphabet positions. A memory aid is E J O T Y, which are five, ten, fifteen, twenty and twenty five. C A T to D B U moves each letter forward by one, so D O G becomes E P H.
Opposite letters add up to 27. || Opposite letters add up to twenty seven. A pairs with Z, B with Y, and M with N. So A B C becomes Z Y X, and G O L D becomes T L O W.
Try this: 3 : 27 :: 4 : ? The answer is A, 64. || Try this. Three is to twenty seven as four is to what? The answer is A, sixty four, because each number is cubed.
Recap. Now try the tools and the practice questions below. || To recap. Say the relationship in a sentence. Try squares and cubes for numbers. Write positions under letters. And always check your rule on both pairs. Now try the tools and the practice questions below this video.
