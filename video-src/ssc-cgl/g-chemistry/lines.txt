# SSC CGL GA — Chemistry
Welcome! This SSC CGL lesson is about chemistry. || Welcome! This S S C C G L general awareness lesson is about chemistry.
Symbols from Latin. || Some symbols come from Latin names. Sodium is N a, potassium is K, iron is F e, gold is A u, silver is A g, lead is P b, and copper is C u.
Common names of compounds. || Learn the common names. Common salt is sodium chloride. Baking soda is sodium bicarbonate, and washing soda is sodium carbonate. Bleaching powder is calcium oxychloride, and dry ice is solid carbon dioxide.
pH: 7 is neutral. || On the p H scale, seven is neutral, like pure water. Below seven is acidic, and above seven is basic. Acids turn blue litmus red, and bases turn red litmus blue.
Acids in everyday life. || Everyday acids: vinegar has acetic acid, lemon has citric acid, and curd has lactic acid.
Air and gases. || Air is about seventy eight percent nitrogen and twenty one percent oxygen. Hydrogen is the lightest gas, and carbon dioxide is used in fire extinguishers.
Metals and alloys. || Mercury is a liquid metal. Rusting needs oxygen and moisture, and galvanising coats iron with zinc. Brass is copper and zinc, and bronze is copper and tin.
Try this: the pH of pure water? The answer is A, 7. || Try this. What is the p H of pure water? The answer is A, seven.
