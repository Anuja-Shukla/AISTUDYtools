# SSC CGL Computer — Data entry speed test
Welcome! This SSC CGL lesson is about the data entry speed test. || Welcome! This S S C C G L lesson is about the data entry speed test, or DEST.
Speed is counted in key depressions per hour. || Speed is counted in key depressions per hour, or K D P H. Every key you press counts, including spaces.
The usual benchmark: 8,000 KDPH. || The usual benchmark is about two thousand key depressions in fifteen minutes, which is eight thousand per hour. Always confirm the exact rule in your exam notice.
Home row: A S D F and J K L. || Learn touch typing. Rest your left fingers on A, S, D and F, and your right fingers on J, K, L and the semicolon. Feel the bumps on F and J to find your place.
Thumbs on the space bar. || Your thumbs press the space bar, and your little fingers handle the shift keys. Keep your eyes on the screen, not on the keys.
Posture. || Sit straight with your feet flat on the floor. Keep your wrists straight and relaxed, and the screen at eye level.
Accuracy first, then speed. || Practise fifteen minutes every day. Aim for accuracy first, ninety eight percent or more, and then raise your speed.
Try this: 2,000 keys in 15 minutes is how many per hour? The answer is A, 8,000. || Try this. Two thousand keys in fifteen minutes is how many per hour? The answer is A, eight thousand.
