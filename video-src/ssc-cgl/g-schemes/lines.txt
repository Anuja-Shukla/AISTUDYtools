# SSC CGL GA — Government schemes
Welcome! This SSC CGL lesson is about government schemes. || Welcome! This S S C C G L general awareness lesson is about government schemes.
Name, aim, year, who it helps. || Learn each scheme in four parts: its name, its aim, its launch year, and who it helps. Launch years and aims stay fixed, but amounts can change, so check official sites before your exam.
2014: Jan Dhan, Make in India, Swachh Bharat. || In twenty fourteen came Jan Dhan Yojana, for bank accounts for all households, Make in India, for manufacturing, and Swachh Bharat Mission, launched on Gandhi Jayanti.
2015: Beti Bachao, Mudra, Digital India. || In twenty fifteen came Beti Bachao Beti Padhao, for the girl child, Mudra Yojana, for small business loans, Atal Pension Yojana, and Digital India.
2016: Ujjwala and Startup India. || In twenty sixteen came Startup India, Stand Up India, and Ujjwala Yojana, which gives L P G connections to women in poor households.
2018–2019: Ayushman Bharat, PM-KISAN, Jal Jeevan. || Ayushman Bharat P M J A Y came in twenty eighteen, with health cover of five lakh rupees per family at launch. P M KISAN and Jal Jeevan Mission came in twenty nineteen.
MGNREGA: up to 100 days of work. || The older M G N R E G A, an Act of two thousand and five, offers up to one hundred days of paid work a year to rural households.
Try this: which scheme gives LPG connections? The answer is A, Ujjwala. || Try this. Which scheme gives L P G connections to women in poor households? The answer is A, P M Ujjwala Yojana.
