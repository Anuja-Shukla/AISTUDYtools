# SSC CGL English — Sentence improvement
Welcome! This SSC CGL lesson is about sentence improvement. || Welcome! This S S C C G L English lesson is about sentence improvement.
Replace the marked part, or choose no improvement. || One part of the sentence is marked. Choose the option that corrects it, or choose no improvement if it is already right.
Clashing pairs: unless … not, although … but. || Watch for clashing pairs. Unless already means if not, so unless you do not work is wrong. Say unless you work. And although never takes but.
too … to, but so … that. || Use too with to, and so with that. He is so weak that he cannot walk, not too weak that. Hardly goes with when, and no sooner goes with than.
Verb forms after did and if. || After did, use the base verb: she did not come. In an imaginary condition, use were: if I were a bird. With yesterday, use the simple past.
Word patterns. || Learn the patterns. Insist on going. Prefer coffee to tea. Senior to me. And use to buy for purpose, not for buying.
Put each option back into the sentence. || To check, put each option back into the sentence and read it again. The right one sounds natural and breaks no rule.
Try this: unless you do not work hard. The answer is A, unless you work hard. || Try this. Unless you do not work hard, you will fail. The answer is A, unless you work hard, because unless already means if not.
