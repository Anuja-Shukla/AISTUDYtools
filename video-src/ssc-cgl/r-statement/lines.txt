# SSC CGL Reasoning — Statement and conclusion
Welcome! This SSC CGL lesson is about statements and conclusions. || Welcome! This S S C C G L lesson is about statements, conclusions, assumptions and courses of action.
Conclusion, assumption, course of action. || A conclusion must be true if the statement is true. An assumption is what the speaker takes for granted. A course of action is a sensible step to deal with a problem.
Stay inside the statement. || The golden rule is to stay inside the statement. All students above ninety percent get a scholarship, and Riya scored ninety five. So Riya gets a scholarship. But only Riya gets one is a new claim, so it does not follow.
Watch the strong words. || Watch the strong words: all, only, always, never, must and none. A conclusion with a strong word follows only if the statement is just as strong. Most students failed does not mean no student passed.
Assumptions: would the statement make sense without it? || For assumptions, ask whether the statement would still make sense without it. Please use the stairs, the lift is under repair, assumes people can use the stairs. It does not assume the lift will never work again.
Courses of action: practical, helpful, not extreme. || A course of action must be practical, it must help with the problem, and it must not be extreme. If tap water is making people ill, test the supply. Do not ask the whole town to leave.
Try this: some guests are vegetarians. The answer is A, either I or II. || Try this. Some guests are vegetarians. Conclusion one, some guests are not vegetarians. Conclusion two, all guests are vegetarians. The answer is A, either one or two, because together they cover every case.
Recap. Now try the tools and the practice questions below. || To recap. Stay inside the statement. Reject extreme words. Test assumptions by removing them. And choose actions that are practical and not extreme. Now try the tools and the practice questions below this video.
