# SSC CGL English — Fill in the blanks
Welcome! This SSC CGL lesson is about fill in the blanks. || Welcome! This S S C C G L English lesson is about fill in the blanks.
Every blank has a clue in the sentence. || Every blank has a clue somewhere in the sentence. It may be a word that takes a fixed preposition, a time word, a contrast word, or a fixed pair.
Fixed prepositions: good at, fond of, insist on. || Learn the fixed prepositions. Good at, fond of, insist on, accused of, interested in, and prefer something to something else.
Articles go by sound. || Articles go by sound, not spelling. A university and a European, because they start with a yoo sound. An hour and an honest man, because the h is silent. And an S S C exam, because S is said es.
Since a point, for a period. || For time, since takes a point of time, like since Monday. For takes a period, like for ten years. Already is used in positive sentences, and yet in questions and negatives.
Fixed pairs: neither nor, so that, no sooner than. || Learn the fixed pairs. Neither goes with nor, so goes with that, and no sooner goes with than. Despite is followed by a noun, but although is followed by a full clause.
When grammar fits, decide on meaning. || When every option fits the grammar, decide on meaning. Despite his hard work, he failed. Despite signals a contrast. And remember, affect is a verb and effect is a noun.
Try this: he is good ___ mathematics. The answer is A, at. || Try this. He is good blank mathematics. The answer is A, at, because good always takes at.
Recap. Now try the tools and the practice questions below. || To recap. Find the clue word. Learn fixed prepositions and pairs. Choose articles by sound. And use meaning when grammar is not enough. Now try the tools and the practice questions below this video.
