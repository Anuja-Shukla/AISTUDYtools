# SSC CGL Reasoning — Order and ranking
Welcome! This SSC CGL lesson is about order and ranking. || Welcome! This S S C C G L lesson is about order and ranking.
One formula: left + right − 1 = total. || Almost every question uses one formula. Position from the left, plus position from the right, minus one, equals the total. We subtract one because the person is counted from both ends.
7th from top, 18th from bottom → 24. || If Ravi is seventh from the top and eighteenth from the bottom, the class has seven plus eighteen minus one, which is twenty four students.
Other end = total − position + 1. || To find the position from the other end, take the total, subtract the position, and add one. In a row of forty, fifteenth from the left is twenty sixth from the right.
Between: convert to the same end, then subtract 1. || To count people between two persons, first change both positions to the same end. Then take the difference and subtract one.
Interchange: one takes the other's old place. || In interchange questions, after the swap, each person stands in the other's old place. If Riya becomes eighteenth from the left, Meena was eighteenth from the left. With Meena eighth from the right, the total is twenty five.
Comparisons: write one chain. || For comparisons, write one chain with the tallest on the left. D is taller than A, A than B, B than C, and C than E. So D is the tallest, E is the shortest, and B is in the middle.
Try this: 13th from both ends. The answer is A, 25. || Try this. A girl is thirteenth from both ends of a row. How many are in the row? The answer is A, twenty five, because two times thirteen minus one is twenty five.
