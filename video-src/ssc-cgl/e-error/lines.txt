# SSC CGL English — Spot the error
Welcome! This SSC CGL lesson is about spotting the error. || Welcome! This S S C C G L English lesson is about spotting the error.
One part is wrong, or there is no error. || The sentence is split into three parts and a no error option. Read the whole sentence first, then test each part against a short list of rules.
Each, every, one of: singular verb. || Rule one is subject verb agreement. Each, every and one of take a singular verb. Each of the boys has finished, not have finished.
Neither…nor: the nearer subject decides. || With neither nor, the verb agrees with the nearer subject. Neither the teacher nor the students were present. With as well as, the first subject decides.
No double comparatives; prefer … to. || Rule two is comparison. Never say more cleverer or most tallest. And remember, prefer takes to, not than. She prefers tea to coffee.
Tenses: since, for, and past time words. || Rule three is tenses. Since takes a point of time, and for takes a period. With yesterday or last week, use the simple past. He returned last week, not he has returned.
Articles by sound; no preposition after discuss. || Rule four. Use a before a consonant sound, so a useful idea and a university. Use an before a vowel sound, like an hour. And never say discuss about or reach at.
Try this: the news are very good. The answer is A, are. || Try this. The news are very good today. Which part is wrong? The answer is A, are. News is uncountable, so it takes is.
Recap. Now try the tools and the practice questions below. || To recap. Find the subject and its verb first. Then check comparisons, tenses and articles. And remember that no error is a real answer. Now try the tools and the practice questions below this video.
