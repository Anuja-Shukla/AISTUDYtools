# SSC CGL Quant — Partnership
Welcome! This SSC CGL lesson is about partnership. || Welcome! This S S C C G L lesson is about partnership, which is ratio applied to money and time.
Same time: profit is shared in the ratio of capital. || When partners invest for the same time, the profit is shared in the ratio of their capital. Thirty thousand and forty five thousand is two to three. A profit of fifteen thousand gives six thousand and nine thousand.
Different times: multiply capital by months. || When the times differ, multiply each capital by its months. Forty thousand for twelve months is four eighty. Sixty thousand for six months is three sixty. So the profit ratio is four to three.
Joined after x months means in for 12 − x months. || If B joins after four months, B is in the business for eight months, not four. Fifty thousand times twelve, and sixty thousand times eight, give five to four.
Changing capital: add each block of capital × months. || If capital changes during the year, add each block separately. Thirty thousand for six months plus twenty thousand for six months is three hundred, the same as twenty five thousand for twelve months.
Working partner: pay the salary first, then share the rest. || A working partner may get a salary or commission. Pay that first, then share the rest. Ten percent of fifty thousand is five thousand. The other forty five thousand is shared three to two.
Missing time: time ratio = profit ratio ÷ capital ratio. || To find a missing time, divide the profit ratio by the capital ratio. If capitals are three to five and profits nine to ten, the times are three to two.
Try this: A 50,000 for 12 months, B 60,000 for 8. The answer is A, 5 : 4. || Try this. A invests fifty thousand for twelve months, and B sixty thousand for eight months. What is the profit ratio? The answer is A, five to four. Six hundred to four hundred and eighty.
