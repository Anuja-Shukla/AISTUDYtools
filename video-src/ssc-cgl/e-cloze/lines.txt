# SSC CGL English — Cloze test
Welcome! This SSC CGL lesson is about the cloze test. || Welcome! This S S C C G L English lesson is about the cloze test.
Read the whole passage first. || A cloze test is a passage with numbered blanks. Read the whole passage once before you fill anything. Find the topic and the tone, because many blanks depend on what comes later.
The neighbours decide the form. || The words around a blank decide its form. After a or the, you need a noun or an adjective. After can, will or to, you need the base verb. After has or have, you need the past participle.
-ing after stop and enjoy. || After stop, enjoy and avoid, use the ing form. We must stop dumping waste into rivers.
Fixed partners. || Learn fixed partners. Depend on. Too late to. So patient that. One of the most. And a few with countable nouns.
Meaning and linkers. || Then use meaning. However and while show contrast. So shows a result. And the logic of the passage tells you that healthy habits make illness less likely.
Fill the easy ones first. || Fill the easy grammar blanks first, then the meaning blanks. Finally, read the whole passage once more with your answers in place.
Try this: only a ___ students joined. The answer is A, few. || Try this. Only a blank students joined. The answer is A, few, because students can be counted.
Recap. Now try the tools and the practice questions below. || To recap. Skim the passage. Let the neighbouring words decide the form. Use fixed partners and linkers. And reread at the end. Now try the tools and the practice questions below this video.
