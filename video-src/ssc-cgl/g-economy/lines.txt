# SSC CGL GA — Indian economy
Welcome! This SSC CGL lesson is about the Indian economy. || Welcome! This S S C C G L general awareness lesson is about the Indian economy.
Learn the basic terms exactly. || Learn the basic terms exactly. G D P is the value of all final goods and services produced within a country in a year. Inflation is a general rise in prices.
Fiscal deficit and stagflation. || Fiscal deficit is total expenditure minus total receipts, excluding borrowings. Stagflation is high inflation together with slow growth.
Three sectors. || The primary sector uses natural resources, like farming and mining. The secondary sector makes things, like factories. The tertiary sector provides services, like banking and transport.
The RBI: set up in 1935, in Mumbai. || The Reserve Bank of India was set up in nineteen thirty five and is headquartered in Mumbai. It issues all currency notes except the one rupee note.
Repo, reverse repo, CRR, SLR. || The repo rate is the rate at which the R B I lends to banks. The reverse repo rate is what it pays banks for parking money. Raising these rates makes loans costlier and helps control inflation.
Taxes and key years. || Income tax is a direct tax. G S T is an indirect tax, and it came into force on the first of July twenty seventeen. N I T I Aayog replaced the Planning Commission in twenty fifteen.
Try this: the repo rate is the rate at which? The answer is A. || Try this. The repo rate is the rate at which the R B I lends to commercial banks. That is answer A.
