# SSC CGL Quant — Simplification & BODMAS
Welcome! This SSC CGL lesson is about simplification and BODMAS. || Welcome! This S S C C G L lesson is about simplification and the BODMAS rule, some of the quickest marks in the paper.
The order: vinculum, brackets, of, then ÷ and ×, then + and −. || Always simplify in this order. First the vinculum, the bar over numbers. Then brackets: round first, then curly, then square. Then of. Then division and multiplication. Then addition and subtraction.
Division and multiplication have equal rank: go left to right. || Division and multiplication have equal rank, so go from left to right. Eighteen divided by three is six. Six times two is twelve. Twelve plus four minus six gives ten. Division does not jump the queue.
“Of” means multiply, but it comes before division. || Of means multiply, but it comes before division and multiplication. So one thousand and one, divided by eleven of thirteen, is one thousand and one divided by one hundred and forty three, which is seven.
Recurring decimals: one 9 for each repeating digit, one 0 for each digit that doesn't. || For a recurring decimal, put one nine for each repeating digit. Zero point three six recurring is thirty six over ninety nine, which is four elevenths. If only some digits repeat, add one zero for each digit that does not repeat. Zero point one six, with only six repeating, is fifteen over ninety, or one sixth.
Four speed tricks: near 100, a² − b², × 99 and × 25. || Four speed tricks. Ninety eight times one hundred and two is a hundred squared minus two squared. Forty seven times fifty three is fifty squared minus three squared. To multiply by ninety nine, multiply by a hundred and subtract once. To multiply by twenty five, multiply by a hundred and divide by four.
For “approximately”, round every number first. || When the question says approximately, round every number first. Thirty nine point nine seven percent of six hundred becomes forty percent of six hundred, which is two hundred and forty. Add fifteen times eight, one hundred and twenty. The answer is about three hundred and sixty.
Try this: 5 + 5 × 5 − 5 ÷ 5. The answer is A, 29. || Try this. Five plus five times five, minus five divided by five. The answer is A, twenty nine. Five times five is twenty five, five divided by five is one, so five plus twenty five minus one is twenty nine.
