# SSC CGL Quant — Profit, loss & discount
Welcome! This SSC CGL lesson is about profit, loss and discount. || Welcome! This S S C C G L lesson is about profit, loss and discount, one of the highest scoring arithmetic topics.
Three prices: cost, marked and selling. Profit is on CP; discount is on MP. || There are three prices. The cost price, the marked price on the tag, and the selling price the buyer pays. Profit and loss are percentages of the cost price. Discount is a percentage of the marked price.
Profit % = profit ÷ CP × 100. Use multiplying factors. || Profit percent is profit divided by cost price, times a hundred. Bought for four hundred, sold for five hundred, is a twenty five percent profit. To go back from S P to C P, divide by the factor. Six hundred and ninety at fifteen percent profit means a cost of six hundred.
Mark up 40%, discount 25%: profit is 5%, not 15%. || A trader marks goods forty percent above cost and gives twenty five percent off. One hundred becomes one hundred and forty, then one hundred and five. The profit is only five percent, because the discount is taken on the bigger marked price.
Successive discounts: 20% and 10% make 28%, not 30%. || Two discounts are applied one after the other. Twenty percent and ten percent make twenty eight percent, not thirty. Use d one plus d two, minus d one times d two over a hundred.
Same SP, x% gain and x% loss: always a loss of x²/100 %. || If two items are sold at the same price, one at ten percent gain and one at ten percent loss, the result is always a loss. The loss is x squared over a hundred, here one percent.
False weights and free offers. || A dealer who gives nine hundred grams for a kilogram makes one hundred over nine hundred, about eleven percent. And buy three get one free is a discount of one in four, twenty five percent.
Try this: CP of 12 = SP of 10. The answer is A, 20%. || Try this. The cost price of twelve articles equals the selling price of ten. What is the profit percent? The answer is A, twenty percent. Two extra, divided by ten.
