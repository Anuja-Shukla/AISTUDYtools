# SSC CGL Quant — Percentages
Welcome! This SSC CGL lesson is about percentages, one of the most scored topics in Quant. || Welcome! This S S C C G L lesson is about percentages, one of the highest scoring topics in quantitative aptitude.
Per cent means per hundred. 20% of 450 is 90. || Per cent means per hundred. So x percent of y is x divided by a hundred, times y. Twenty percent of four hundred and fifty is ninety. A handy trick: x percent of y equals y percent of x.
Learn these fraction values by heart; they save time in every question. || Learn these fraction values by heart. One half is fifty percent, one third is thirty three and a third, one eighth is twelve and a half. They save time in almost every question.
Percentage change = change ÷ old value × 100. From 80 to 100 is a 25% rise. || Percentage change is the change, divided by the old value, times a hundred. If a price rises from eighty to a hundred rupees, the rise is twenty on eighty, which is twenty five percent.
A 20% rise then a 20% fall is a net 4% loss, because the base changes. || Watch the base. A twenty percent rise followed by a twenty percent fall gives a net loss of four percent. And if A is twenty five percent more than B, then B is only twenty percent less than A.
Two changes in a row: a + b + ab/100. +10% then +20% gives 32%. || For two changes in a row, use a plus b plus a b over a hundred. A ten percent rise, then a twenty percent rise, gives a thirty two percent rise overall.
Three shortcuts: multiplying factors, the fixed-spending rule, and growth over n years. || Three shortcuts. Use multiplying factors, like one point one five for a fifteen percent rise. To keep spending fixed when price rises by r percent, cut use by r over a hundred plus r. And for growth over n years, multiply by one plus r over a hundred, to the power n.
Try this: sugar costs 25% more. The answer is A, 20%. || Try this. Sugar becomes twenty five percent costlier. By how much must a family cut consumption to keep spending the same? The answer is A, twenty percent, because twenty five over one hundred and twenty five is one fifth.
