# SSC CGL GA — Current affairs
Welcome! This SSC CGL lesson is about current affairs. || Welcome! This S S C C G L general awareness lesson is about current affairs.
Questions come from the last 6 to 12 months. || Current affairs questions usually come from the six to twelve months before your exam. They cover appointments, awards, sports, indices, schemes, science and summits.
A 15-minute daily method. || Spend fifteen minutes a day. Read one reliable source, such as P I B or a national newspaper. Write only exam type facts: who, what, where and when.
Tag, revise weekly, summarise monthly. || Tag each note, such as award or sports. Revise the week every Sunday, and make a one page summary every month.
Headquarters behind the news. || Many questions lean on stable facts. The U N and UNICEF are in New York. W H O, W T O and the I L O are in Geneva. The I M F and the World Bank are in Washington.
Who publishes which index. || Know who publishes each index. The Human Development Index comes from U N D P. The Global Gender Gap Report comes from the World Economic Forum. The World Economic Outlook comes from the I M F.
Regional bodies. || ASEAN is in Jakarta, and SAARC is in Kathmandu. The Asian Development Bank is in Manila, and the International Solar Alliance is in Gurugram.
Try this: who publishes the HDI? The answer is A, UNDP. || Try this. Who publishes the Human Development Index? The answer is A, U N D P.
