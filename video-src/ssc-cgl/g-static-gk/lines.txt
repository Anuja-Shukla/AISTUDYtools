# SSC CGL GA — Static GK
Welcome! This SSC CGL lesson is about static GK. || Welcome! This S S C C G L general awareness lesson is about static G K.
Facts that do not change. || Static G K covers facts that do not change: important days, books and authors, national symbols, awards and sports terms. Steady revision pays off.
Important days. || Learn the important days. National Science Day is twenty eight February. World Environment Day is five June. International Yoga Day is twenty one June. National Sports Day is twenty nine August.
More days. || Teachers' Day is five September. Children's Day is fourteen November. And Constitution Day is twenty six November.
Books and authors. || Learn books as pairs. Gitanjali by Rabindranath Tagore. Godaan by Munshi Premchand. The Discovery of India by Jawaharlal Nehru. And Wings of Fire by A P J Abdul Kalam.
National symbols. || India's national animal is the tiger, the national bird is the peacock, and the national flower is the lotus. The national aquatic animal is the Ganges river dolphin.
Awards and sports. || The Bharat Ratna is the highest civilian award. The Arjuna Award honours sportspersons, and the Dronacharya Award honours coaches. Checkmate is from chess, and bogey is from golf.
