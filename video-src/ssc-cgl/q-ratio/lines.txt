# SSC CGL Quant — Ratio & proportion
Welcome! This SSC CGL lesson is about ratio and proportion. || Welcome! This S S C C G L lesson is about ratio and proportion, the engine behind partnership, mixtures and ages.
A ratio compares quantities in the same units. Think in parts. || A ratio compares two quantities of the same kind, in the same units. Forty five minutes to two hours is forty five to one hundred and twenty, or three to eight. Think of a ratio as parts of equal size.
To share an amount: add the parts, find one part, multiply. || To share an amount, add the parts, find one part, then multiply. Eighteen hundred rupees in the ratio two to three to four is nine parts. One part is two hundred, so the shares are four hundred, six hundred and eight hundred.
To join A to B and B to C, make B the same. || To join A to B with B to C, make B the same. If A to B is two to three, and B to C is four to five, the L C M of three and four is twelve. So A to B to C is eight to twelve to fifteen.
In a proportion, extremes multiplied equal means multiplied. || In a proportion, the product of the extremes equals the product of the means. The fourth proportional to a, b, c is b c over a. The third proportional is b squared over a. And the mean proportional is the square root of a b.
Adding to change a ratio: set up the equation, or test the options. || What number added to seven and eleven gives the ratio three to four? Seven plus x over eleven plus x equals three over four, so x is five. Check: twelve to sixteen is three to four. In the exam, testing the options is often faster.
Direct and inverse proportion. Men × days stays the same. || In direct proportion, both quantities rise together. In inverse proportion, one rises as the other falls. If twelve men take ten days, fifteen men take twelve times ten divided by fifteen, which is eight days.
Try this: 96 in the ratio 5 : 7. The answer is A, 56. || Try this. Two numbers in the ratio five to seven add up to ninety six. What is the larger number? The answer is A, fifty six. Twelve parts make ninety six, so one part is eight, and seven parts are fifty six.
