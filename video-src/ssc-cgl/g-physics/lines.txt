# SSC CGL GA — Physics
Welcome! This SSC CGL lesson is about physics. || Welcome! This S S C C G L general awareness lesson is about physics.
Learn facts as pairs. || Physics questions in general awareness are short facts. Learn them as pairs: a quantity and its unit, a defect and its lens, an effect and its example.
SI units. || The unit of force is the newton. Work and energy are measured in joules, and power in watts. Current is measured in amperes, resistance in ohms, and frequency in hertz.
Newton's laws and gravity. || Newton's first law is the law of inertia. The second law says force equals mass times acceleration. And on Earth, the acceleration due to gravity is about nine point eight metres per second squared.
Light: dispersion, scattering, lenses. || The speed of light is about three times ten to the power eight metres per second. A rainbow is caused by dispersion, and the blue sky by scattering. A concave lens corrects short sight, and a convex lens corrects long sight.
Sound needs a medium. || Sound needs a medium, so it cannot travel through a vacuum. Sound waves are longitudinal, and sound travels fastest in solids.
Electricity and instruments. || A fuse works on the heating effect of current. A generator turns motion into electricity, and a motor does the reverse. A barometer measures air pressure, and a hygrometer measures humidity.
Try this: the SI unit of power is? The answer is A, watt. || Try this. What is the S I unit of power? The answer is A, the watt.
