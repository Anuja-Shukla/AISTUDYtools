# SSC CGL English — Active and passive voice
Welcome! This SSC CGL lesson is about active and passive voice. || Welcome! This S S C C G L English lesson is about active and passive voice.
Three steps: object first, be plus V3, then by. || Changing to passive takes three steps. The object becomes the new subject. The verb becomes a form of be in the same tense plus the past participle. And the doer moves after by. Ram writes a letter becomes a letter is written by Ram.
Keep the tense the same. || The tense must stay the same. Simple present uses is, am or are. Simple past uses was or were. Future uses will be.
Continuous adds being; perfect adds been. || For continuous tenses, add being: dinner is being cooked. For perfect tenses, add been: the work has been finished.
Pronouns change form. || Pronouns change form. I becomes me, he becomes him, she becomes her, and they becomes them. He will help me becomes I will be helped by him.
Commands use let; who becomes by whom. || Commands use let. Close the door becomes let the door be closed. Questions with who start with by whom: by whom was this book written?
Drop an unknown doer. || If the doer is unknown or general, drop it. Someone has stolen my bag becomes my bag has been stolen.
Try this: she is cooking dinner. The answer is A. || Try this. She is cooking dinner. The answer is A: dinner is being cooked by her, because the present continuous needs is being.
