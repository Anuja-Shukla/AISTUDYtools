# SSC CGL Reasoning — Mirror and water images
Welcome! This SSC CGL lesson is about mirror and water images. || Welcome! This S S C C G L lesson is about mirror and water images.
Mirror: left ↔ right. Water: top ↔ bottom. || A mirror placed at the side swaps left and right, but the top stays on top. Water below an object swaps top and bottom, but left stays on the left.
Mirror-safe letters: A H I M O T U V W X Y. || Some capital letters do not change in a mirror: A, H, I, M, O, T, U, V, W, X and Y. In water, these stay the same: B, C, D, E, H, I, K, O and X.
Words: reverse the order and flip each letter. || A mirror reverses the order of the letters and flips each one. So a word looks the same only if it reads the same backwards and uses only mirror safe letters, like MOM or WOW.
Mirror clocks: 11:60 − time. || For clocks in a mirror, subtract the time from eleven sixty. Three forty becomes eight twenty. The rule works both ways, so the mirror of eight twenty is three forty.
Special cases: 12:00 and 6:00 stay the same. || Twelve o'clock and six o'clock look the same in a mirror. For twelve twenty, treat it as zero twenty. Eleven sixty minus zero twenty is eleven forty.
Figures: follow one detail. || For figures, follow one small detail, like a dot or a corner, and see where it moves. In a mirror, an arrow pointing right now points left.
Try this: a mirror shows 9:10. The answer is A, 2:50. || Try this. A clock seen in a mirror shows nine ten. What is the real time? The answer is A, two fifty, because eleven sixty minus nine ten is two fifty.
