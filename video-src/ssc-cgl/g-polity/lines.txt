# SSC CGL GA — Indian polity
Welcome! This SSC CGL lesson is about Indian polity. || Welcome! This S S C C G L general awareness lesson is about Indian polity.
Adopted 26 November 1949; in force 26 January 1950. || The Constitution was adopted on twenty six November nineteen forty nine, and came into force on twenty six January nineteen fifty. Dr B R Ambedkar chaired the Drafting Committee.
Part III rights, Part IV directive principles. || Learn the key parts. Part three has the Fundamental Rights. Part four has the Directive Principles. Part four A has the Fundamental Duties.
Key rights: Articles 14, 17, 21, 32. || Key rights: Article fourteen, equality before law. Article seventeen, abolition of untouchability. Article twenty one, life and personal liberty. Article thirty two, constitutional remedies, called the heart and soul of the Constitution.
Duties: added in 1976, now 11. || The Fundamental Duties were added by the forty second amendment in nineteen seventy six. There are now eleven.
Ages: 25, 30, 35. || Remember the ages. Twenty five for the Lok Sabha, thirty for the Rajya Sabha, and thirty five for the President.
Emergencies: 352, 356, 360. || Three emergency articles. Three fifty two is a national emergency. Three fifty six is President's Rule in a state. Three sixty is a financial emergency. And Article three sixty eight is for amending the Constitution.
Try this: Article 21 deals with? The answer is A. || Try this. What does Article twenty one deal with? The answer is A, protection of life and personal liberty.
