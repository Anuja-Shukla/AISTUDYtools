# SSC CGL English — Reading comprehension
Welcome! This SSC CGL lesson is about reading comprehension. || Welcome! This S S C C G L English lesson is about reading comprehension.
Read in two passes. || Read the passage in two passes. In the first pass, read quickly for the topic and the main idea. In the second pass, go back to the exact lines that each question points to.
Five question types. || There are five common question types: main idea, detail, inference, vocabulary in context, and tone. Each one needs a slightly different approach.
Main idea covers the whole passage. || The main idea must cover the whole passage. Reject options that are only one detail, that go beyond the passage, or that reverse the writer's view.
Detail: find the keyword. || For detail questions, find the question's keyword in the passage and read that sentence. The right answer is usually the same idea in different words.
Inference: it must follow from the text. || For inference questions, the answer is not written directly, but it must clearly follow from the text. Reject anything that needs outside facts.
Vocabulary: replace and reread. || For vocabulary questions, put each option in place of the word and reread the sentence. Choose the meaning that fits this passage.
Keep your opinions out. || Most importantly, keep your own opinions out. Only the passage counts. And be careful with extreme words like always, never and all.
