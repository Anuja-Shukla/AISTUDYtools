# SSC CGL GA — History and culture
Welcome! This SSC CGL lesson is about history and culture. || Welcome! This S S C C G L general awareness lesson is about Indian history and culture.
Three periods: ancient, medieval, modern. || History questions come from three periods: ancient, medieval and modern. Build one timeline and add people and places to it.
Harappa: Mohenjo-daro's Great Bath, Lothal's dockyard. || The Harappan civilisation had planned cities. Mohenjo-daro had the Great Bath, and Lothal in Gujarat had a dockyard.
Mauryas and Guptas. || Chandragupta Maurya founded the Maurya Empire, and Kautilya wrote the Arthashastra. Ashoka turned to Buddhism after the Kalinga war. Samudragupta is called the Napoleon of India.
Medieval: Sultanate and Mughals. || The Delhi Sultanate began in twelve oh six. In the first battle of Panipat in fifteen twenty six, Babur defeated Ibrahim Lodi. Shah Jahan built the Taj Mahal.
British rule: Plassey 1757, Revolt 1857. || The Battle of Plassey in seventeen fifty seven gave the East India Company power in Bengal. The Revolt of eighteen fifty seven began at Meerut. The Congress was founded in eighteen eighty five.
Freedom struggle dates. || Learn the freedom struggle dates. Jallianwala Bagh in nineteen nineteen. Dandi March in nineteen thirty. Quit India, with the call do or die, in nineteen forty two. Independence in nineteen forty seven.
Try this: the Dandi March began in which year? The answer is A, 1930. || Try this. In which year did the Dandi March begin? The answer is A, nineteen thirty.
