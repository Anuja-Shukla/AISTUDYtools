# SSC CGL Quant — Geometry
Welcome! This SSC CGL lesson is about geometry. || Welcome! This S S C C G L lesson is about geometry, one of the biggest areas in the exam.
Parallel lines: corresponding and alternate angles are equal; co-interior angles add to 180°. || When a line cuts two parallel lines, corresponding angles and alternate angles are equal, and co-interior angles add up to one hundred and eighty degrees.
Triangle angles add to 180°. The exterior angle equals the two opposite interior angles. || The angles of a triangle add to one hundred and eighty. An exterior angle equals the sum of the two opposite interior angles.
Pythagoras: learn the triplets 3-4-5, 5-12-13, 8-15-17 and 7-24-25. || In a right triangle, the hypotenuse squared equals the sum of the squares of the other two sides. Learn the triplets: three four five, five twelve thirteen, eight fifteen seventeen, and seven twenty four twenty five.
Four centres: centroid 2 : 1, incentre 90 + A/2, orthocentre 180 − A, circumcentre 2A. || Learn the four centres. The centroid divides each median two to one. At the incentre, angle B I C is ninety plus half of A. At the orthocentre, angle B H C is one eighty minus A. At the circumcentre, angle B O C is two A.
Circles: the angle at the centre is twice the angle at the circumference. || In a circle, the angle at the centre is twice the angle at the circumference on the same arc. The angle in a semicircle is ninety degrees, and opposite angles of a cyclic quadrilateral add to one eighty.
Tangent length √(d² − r²); half-chord √(r² − d²). || A tangent meets the radius at ninety degrees. So the tangent length from a point is the square root of d squared minus r squared. For a chord, the half chord is the square root of r squared minus d squared.
Try this: ∠A = 70°, find ∠BIC at the incentre. The answer is A, 125°. || Try this. In triangle A B C, angle A is seventy degrees. What is angle B I C at the incentre? The answer is A, one hundred and twenty five degrees. Ninety plus thirty five.
