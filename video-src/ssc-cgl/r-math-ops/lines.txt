# SSC CGL Reasoning — Mathematical operations
Welcome! This SSC CGL lesson is about mathematical operations. || Welcome! This S S C C G L lesson is about mathematical operations.
Changed meanings, interchanges and hidden rules. || There are three types: signs with changed meanings, signs to interchange, and hidden rules like two star three equals thirteen. All three end with BODMAS.
BODMAS: brackets, of, divide and multiply, add and subtract. || Remember BODMAS. Brackets, then of, then division and multiplication from left to right, then addition and subtraction from left to right. Eighteen minus twelve divided by four times two plus three is fifteen.
Changed meanings: rewrite the whole line first. || When signs have new meanings, rewrite the whole line before you calculate. If plus means times, times means minus, minus means divide and divide means plus, then eight plus four times six minus two divide five becomes eight times four minus six divided by two plus five, which is thirty four.
Interchange: test each option. || For interchange questions, test each option. To make twelve divided by four plus three times two equal eleven, swap plus and times. Twelve divided by four times three plus two is eleven.
Hidden rules: test on both examples. || For hidden rules, test common patterns on both examples. Two star three is thirteen, and four star five is forty one. That is a squared plus b squared. So three star four is twenty five.
Try this: 5 # 3 = 16, 7 # 2 = 45. 6 # 4 = ? The answer is A, 20. || Try this. If five hash three is sixteen and seven hash two is forty five, what is six hash four? The answer is A, twenty, because the rule is a squared minus b squared.
