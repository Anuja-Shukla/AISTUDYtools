# SSC CGL GA — Biology
Welcome! This SSC CGL lesson is about biology. || Welcome! This S S C C G L general awareness lesson is about biology.
The cell is the basic unit of life. || The cell is the basic unit of life. Mitochondria are the powerhouse of the cell, and chloroplasts carry out photosynthesis in plants.
Vitamins and deficiency diseases. || Learn the vitamin table. Lack of vitamin A causes night blindness. Lack of B one causes beriberi. Lack of vitamin C causes scurvy. And lack of vitamin D causes rickets.
Fat soluble: A, D, E, K. Water soluble: B, C. || The skin makes vitamin D in sunlight. Vitamins A, D, E and K are fat soluble, while the B group and C are water soluble.
Blood: bone marrow and iron. || Red blood cells are made in the bone marrow, and haemoglobin contains iron. O negative is the universal donor, and A B positive is the universal recipient.
Body numbers. || An adult has two hundred and six bones. The smallest is the stapes in the ear. The skin is the largest organ, and the liver is the largest gland. The heart has four chambers.
Common diseases. || Malaria is caused by Plasmodium and spread by the female Anopheles mosquito. Dengue is a virus spread by the Aedes mosquito. Insulin from the pancreas controls blood sugar.
Try this: lack of vitamin C causes? The answer is A, scurvy. || Try this. Lack of vitamin C causes which disease? The answer is A, scurvy.
