# SSC CGL English — Synonyms and antonyms
Welcome! This SSC CGL lesson is about synonyms and antonyms. || Welcome! This S S C C G L English lesson is about synonyms and antonyms.
Synonym = same meaning; antonym = opposite. || A synonym has the same meaning, and an antonym has the opposite meaning. The options always include a trap from the other side, so read the instruction carefully.
Learn words in groups. || Learn words in small groups of the same idea. Abundant, ample and plentiful all mean plenty. Candid, frank and sincere all mean honest.
Roots help you guess. || Roots help you guess unknown words. Bene means good, as in benevolent. Mal means bad, as in malice. Loqu means speak, as in eloquent.
Prefixes make opposites. || Prefixes make opposites. Un, dis and in are common. Use im before b, m and p, as in impossible. Use il before l, as in illegal, and ir before r, as in irregular.
Common antonym pairs. || Learn common antonym pairs. Ancient and modern. Hostile and friendly. Expand and contract. Transparent and opaque. Scarce and plentiful.
Make a short sentence with the word. || If you are unsure, make a short sentence with the word. The feeling of the sentence often tells you the meaning.
Try this: the antonym of TIMID. The answer is A, bold. || Try this. Choose the antonym of timid. The answer is A, bold. Timid means shy, so its opposite is bold.
