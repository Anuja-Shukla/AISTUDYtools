# SSC CGL English — Spelling
Welcome! This SSC CGL lesson is about spelling. || Welcome! This S S C C G L English lesson is about spelling.
Watch the double letters. || Most spelling mistakes are missed double letters. Accommodate has two c's and two m's. Embarrass has two r's and two s's. Necessary has one c and two s's.
i before e, except after c. || Remember i before e, except after c. Believe and achieve, but receive and ceiling. Learn the exceptions: seize, weird, either, neither and foreign.
Drop the silent e before a vowel ending. || When you add an ending, drop a silent e before a vowel ending. Hope becomes hoping, but hopeful keeps the e.
y becomes i after a consonant. || After a consonant, y becomes i. Happy becomes happiness, and carry becomes carried. But keep the y before ing: carrying.
Double the final consonant when it is stressed. || Double the final consonant when the last syllable is stressed and ends consonant, vowel, consonant. Run becomes running, and begin becomes beginning. British spelling also doubles l: travelled.
Memory tricks. || Use memory tricks. Separate has a rat in it. Definitely contains finite. And the government governs.
Try this: necessary. The answer is A. || Try this. Which is correct: necessary, neccessary, necesary or neccesary? The answer is A, necessary, with one c and two s's.
