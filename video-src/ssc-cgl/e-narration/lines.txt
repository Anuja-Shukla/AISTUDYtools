# SSC CGL English — Direct and indirect speech
Welcome! This SSC CGL lesson is about direct and indirect speech. || Welcome! This S S C C G L English lesson is about direct and indirect speech.
Four changes. || Changing to indirect speech means four changes: the reporting verb, the tense, the pronouns, and the time and place words.
Tense goes one step back. || If the reporting verb is in the past, the tense moves one step back. Am and is become was. Are becomes were. Has and have become had. Will becomes would, and can becomes could.
Time words change. || Time and place words change too. Now becomes then. Tomorrow becomes the next day. Yesterday becomes the day before. Here becomes there, and this becomes that.
Questions: asked, if, and statement order. || For questions, use asked. Add if for yes or no questions, and keep the wh word otherwise. Use statement order: he asked me where I was going.
Commands: told to; requests: requested to. || For commands, use told and to: he told me to close the window. For negative commands, use not to. For requests with please, use requested.
No change for universal truths. || Universal truths keep their tense. The teacher said that the earth moves round the sun.
Try this: she said, I will come tomorrow. The answer is A. || Try this. She said, I will come tomorrow. The answer is A: she said that she would come the next day.
