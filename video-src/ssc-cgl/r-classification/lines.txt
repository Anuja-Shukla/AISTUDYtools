# SSC CGL Reasoning — Classification
Welcome! This SSC CGL lesson is about classification, or finding the odd one out. || Welcome! This S S C C G L lesson is about classification, where you find the odd one out.
Three items share a property; one does not. || In every question, three items share a property and one does not. The trick is to name the group of three first.
Words: name the category of three. || For words, ask what three of them are. Apple, mango and banana are fruits, so potato is odd. Violin, guitar and sitar have strings, so the flute is odd.
Numbers: test prime, square, cube, then divisibility. || For numbers, test in order: prime, perfect square, cube, even or odd, then divisibility. Seventeen, twenty three and twenty nine are prime, so thirty three is odd.
Letter groups: write the gaps. || For letter groups, write the gaps between the letters. A C E, G I K and M O Q all move by two and two. R T W moves by two and three, so it is the odd one.
Pairs: find the rule in three pairs. || For number pairs, find the rule that fits three of them. Four sixteen, five twenty five and six thirty six follow n squared. Seven forty eight breaks it, because seven squared is forty nine.
Try this: 16, 25, 36, 50. The answer is A, 50. || Try this. Which is the odd one: sixteen, twenty five, thirty six, fifty? The answer is A, fifty, because it is not a perfect square.
