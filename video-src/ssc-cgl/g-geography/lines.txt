# SSC CGL GA — Geography
Welcome! This SSC CGL lesson is about geography. || Welcome! This S S C C G L general awareness lesson is about geography.
India: largest state Rajasthan, smallest Goa. || Start with India. The largest state by area is Rajasthan and the smallest is Goa. The Tropic of Cancer passes through eight states, from Gujarat to Mizoram.
Standard meridian 82.5° East. || Indian Standard Time is based on the eighty two point five degrees east meridian. The southernmost point is Indira Point in the Nicobar Islands.
Rivers: east or west. || Group rivers by where they flow. The Narmada and the Tapi flow west into the Arabian Sea. The Mahanadi, Godavari, Krishna and Kaveri flow east into the Bay of Bengal. The Godavari is called the Dakshin Ganga.
Soils and crops. || Pair soils with crops. Black soil is best for cotton. Alluvial soil makes the northern plains fertile. Laterite soil forms by leaching in hot, wet areas, and red soil gets its colour from iron oxide.
Monsoon and landforms. || Most of India's rain comes from the south west monsoon. The Western Ghats are also called the Sahyadri, and Chilika Lake in Odisha is a large lagoon.
World facts. || For the world, Asia is the largest continent and the Pacific is the largest ocean. Weather happens in the troposphere, and the ozone layer is in the stratosphere.
