# SSC CGL English — One-word substitution
Welcome! This SSC CGL lesson is about one-word substitution. || Welcome! This S S C C G L English lesson is about one word substitution.
Learn word families, not long lists. || You replace a whole phrase with one word. Instead of memorising long lists, learn the word families. Cide means killing, phobia means fear, cracy means rule, and logy means study.
-cide: killing. || With cide: patricide is killing one's father, matricide is killing one's mother, and homicide is killing a human being.
-phobia: fear. || With phobia: acrophobia is fear of heights, hydrophobia is fear of water, and claustrophobia is fear of closed spaces.
Places: aviary, apiary, aquarium, kennel. || Learn the places. An aviary is for birds, an apiary is for bees, an aquarium is for fish, and a kennel is for dogs.
People: polyglot, somnambulist, atheist. || And the people. A polyglot knows many languages. A somnambulist walks in sleep. An atheist does not believe in God.
Watch the look-alikes. || Watch the look alikes. Illegible means handwriting that cannot be read. Ineligible means not qualified. Biography is written by someone else, and autobiography by oneself.
Try this: fear of heights. The answer is A, acrophobia. || Try this. What is the word for fear of heights? The answer is A, acrophobia, because acro means height.
