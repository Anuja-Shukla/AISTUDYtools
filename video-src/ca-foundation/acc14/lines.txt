# Accounting Lesson 1.4 — Contingent Assets and Liabilities
Welcome! This lesson is about contingent assets and liabilities.
Under AS 29: a probable, measurable obligation is a provision; a possible one is a disclosed contingent liability; a remote one is ignored; and a contingent asset is not recognised.
If the company will probably lose a lawsuit and can estimate the loss, it makes a provision. If it only might lose, it discloses a contingent liability.
Common contingent liabilities: claims not acknowledged as debts, guarantees given, bills discounted but not matured, and disputed tax demands.
This is prudence: probable losses are provided for, but gains are recognised only when virtually certain.
