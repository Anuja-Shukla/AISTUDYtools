# Lesson 2.2 — Direction Sense
Welcome! This lesson is about direction sense: directions, turns, distances, and shadows.
There are four main directions: North, East, South and West, and four in between: North-East, South-East, South-West and North-West. Draw them like a compass, with North at the top.
A right turn is a quarter turn clockwise. A left turn is a quarter turn anticlockwise. Both are measured from the way the person is facing now. An about-turn is a half turn.
So facing North, right means East. But facing South, right means West. Never assume that right always means East.
Riya walks 5 km North, turns right and walks 3 km, then turns right again and walks 9 km. Where is she from her starting point? || Riya walks five kilometres North, turns right and walks three kilometres, then turns right again and walks nine kilometres. Where is she from her starting point?
Track it on a grid. North 5 takes her to (0, 5). Facing North, right is East: 3 km to (3, 5). Facing East, right is South: 9 km to (3, −4). || Track it on a grid. North five takes her to zero, five. Facing North, right is East, so three kilometres takes her to three, five. Facing East, right is South, so nine kilometres takes her to three, minus four.
The straight-line distance back is √(3² + 4²) = 5 km. She is East and South of the start, so the answer is 5 km South-East. || The straight-line distance back to the start is the square root of three squared plus four squared, which is five kilometres. She is East and South of the start, so the answer is five kilometres South-East.
Careful: she walked 17 km in total, but she is only 5 km from the start. Questions usually ask for the straight-line distance. || Careful. She walked seventeen kilometres in total, but she is only five kilometres from the start. Questions usually ask for the straight-line distance.
Shadow questions use the sun. It rises in the East and sets in the West, and a shadow points away from the sun. So morning shadows point West, and evening shadows point East.
If your morning shadow falls to your right, which way are you facing? The shadow points West, and West is on your right only when you face South. || If your morning shadow falls to your right, which way are you facing? The shadow points West. And West is on your right only when you are facing South.
Try this: facing West, Asha turns left, then left again. Which way does she face? West → left → South → left → East. || Try this. Facing West, Asha turns left, and then left again. Which way is she facing now? From West, a left turn gives South. Another left turn gives East. The answer is A.
