# Accounting Lesson 1.7 — Accounting Standards
Welcome! This lesson is about accounting standards.
Standards make accounts comparable and reliable by reducing the choice of methods, so users can trust them.
ICAI's Accounting Standards Board drafts a standard, issues an exposure draft, considers comments, and the Council approves it. For companies, standards are notified by the government.
Large and listed companies follow Ind AS, converged with IFRS; other entities follow AS. Company standards are notified under section 133.
In this course you meet AS 1, AS 2, AS 9, AS 10 and AS 29.
