# Business Economics Lesson 4.3 — Budget Making, Revenue, Expenditure and Public Debt
Welcome! This lesson is about budget making, sources of revenue, expenditure and public debt.
The budget, or Annual Financial Statement under Article 112, passes Parliament through demands for grants, the Appropriation Bill and the Finance Bill.
Revenue receipts create no liability; capital receipts, like borrowing and disinvestment, create liabilities or reduce assets.
There are four deficits: revenue, effective revenue, fiscal, and primary.

The fiscal deficit is the government's borrowing. Rising debt brings interest costs and can crowd out private investment.
