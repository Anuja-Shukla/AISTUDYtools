# Business Laws Lesson 2.1 — Performance of Contract
Welcome! This lesson is about performance of contract.
A valid offer to perform must be unconditional, at the right time and place, for the whole obligation, with a chance to inspect. If refused, the promisor is discharged.
With joint promisors, the promisee can make any one pay the whole, and that person claims equal contribution from the rest.
If time is of the essence and missed, the contract is voidable; if not, only compensation is due.
The debtor may direct which debt a payment is for; if he does not, the creditor chooses, even a time-barred debt; if neither does, payments go in order of time.
Kabir pays 30,000 without direction. The creditor may choose; if not, the oldest debt, even time-barred, is cleared first, and 10,000 goes to the next. || Kabir pays thirty thousand without saying which debt. The creditor may choose.
