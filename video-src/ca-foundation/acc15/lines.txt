# Accounting Lesson 1.5 — Accounting Policies
Welcome! This lesson is about accounting policies.
Accounting policies are the specific principles and methods a business chooses, such as FIFO for inventory or straight line depreciation.
Policies are chosen with prudence, substance over form and materiality in mind, and significant policies are disclosed together.
Switching from FIFO to weighted average is a change in policy. Revising a machine's useful life is a change in estimate.
A policy may change only if a statute or standard requires it, or if it gives a more appropriate presentation.
