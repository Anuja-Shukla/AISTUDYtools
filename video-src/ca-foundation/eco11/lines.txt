# Business Economics Lesson 1.1 — Introduction to Business Economics
Welcome! This lesson is about the nature and scope of business economics.
Wants are unlimited and resources scarce, so we must choose, and every choice has an opportunity cost.
Smith defined economics around wealth, Marshall around welfare, Robbins around scarcity and choice, and Samuelson added growth.
Microeconomics studies individual firms and markets; macroeconomics studies the economy as a whole.
Positive statements describe what is and can be tested; normative statements say what ought to be.
