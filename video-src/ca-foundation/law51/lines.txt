# Business Laws Lesson 5.1 — The Companies Act, 2013
Welcome! This lesson is about the Companies Act, 2013.
A company is a separate legal person with limited liability, perpetual succession, its own property, and the capacity to sue.
Courts look behind the company for fraud, sham companies, tax evasion, enemy character, and where statute says so.
A one person company has one member, a private company 2 to 200, and a public company at least 7, with 1, 2 and 3 directors.
A small company has paid-up capital up to 10 crore and turnover up to 100 crore under the current rules. Control decides holding, subsidiary and associate status; 51 percent government holding makes a government company. || A small company has paid-up capital up to ten crore and turnover up to one hundred crore under the current rules. Control decides holding, subsidiary and associate status. And fifty one percent government holding makes a government company.
The memorandum is the charter: acts beyond it are ultra vires and void. The articles are internal rules, and outsiders are protected by the indoor management rule.
