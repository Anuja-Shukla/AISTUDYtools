# Business Economics Lesson 4.4 — Fiscal Policy
Welcome! This lesson is about fiscal policy.
Fiscal policy aims at full employment, stable prices, growth, fairer incomes and external stability.
Expansionary policy raises spending or cuts taxes in a recession; contractionary policy does the reverse to fight inflation.
Discretionary changes need a decision; automatic stabilisers, like progressive taxes, work by themselves.
A gap of 400 crore with MPC 0.75 needs only 100 crore of extra spending, because the multiplier is 4. || To close a gap of four hundred crore when the MPC is zero point seven five, the multiplier is four, so only one hundred crore of extra spending is needed.
Fiscal policy is limited by time lags, crowding out, forecasting problems and rising debt.
