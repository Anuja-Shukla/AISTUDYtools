# Business Economics Lesson 3.3 — Business Cycles
Welcome! This lesson is about business cycles.
A business cycle moves through expansion, peak, contraction and trough, then recovery.
Cycles recur but are not periodic, affect the whole economy, build on themselves, and spread across countries.
Keynes stressed demand, Hawtrey bank credit, Schumpeter innovations, and Pigou waves of optimism and pessimism.
Leading indicators move before the economy, coincident ones with it, and lagging ones after it.
