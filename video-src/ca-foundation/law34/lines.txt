# Business Laws Lesson 3.4 — Unpaid Seller
Welcome! This lesson is about the rights of an unpaid seller.
A seller is unpaid if the whole price is not paid, or a cheque or bill given for it is dishonoured.
If the seller still has the goods, he has a lien; if they are with a carrier and the buyer is insolvent, he can stop them; then he may resell.
The lien is lost by delivery to a carrier without reserving disposal, when the buyer lawfully gets possession, or by waiver; not by obtaining a decree.
The seller may resell perishable goods, after notice if the buyer still does not pay, or where the right was reserved.
He can also sue the buyer for the price, for damages for non-acceptance, and for interest.
