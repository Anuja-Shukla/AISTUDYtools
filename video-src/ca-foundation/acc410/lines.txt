# Accounting Lesson 4.10 — Bonus and Rights Issues
Welcome! This lesson is about bonus and rights issues.
A 1 for 5 bonus on 50,000 shares gives 10,000 new shares: 1 lakh is capitalised from reserves. || A one for five bonus on fifty thousand shares gives ten thousand new shares. One lakh is capitalised from reserves.
Bonus shares can come from free reserves, securities premium or capital redemption reserve, but not from a revaluation reserve.
In a 1 for 4 rights issue at 15 with a market price of 25, the ex-rights price is 23, so each right is worth 2. || In a one for four rights issue at fifteen, with a market price of twenty five, the ex-rights price is twenty three. So each right is worth two rupees.
Bonus shares are free and bring in no cash; rights shares are offered to existing holders for payment.
Try this: bonus shares cannot be issued out of revaluation reserve. || Try this. Bonus shares cannot be issued out of which reserve? The answer is A, revaluation reserve.
