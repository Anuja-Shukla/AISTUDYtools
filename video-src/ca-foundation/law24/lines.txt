# Business Laws Lesson 2.4 — Indemnity and Guarantee
Welcome! This lesson is about indemnity and guarantee.
Indemnity is a promise to save another from loss, with two parties. A guarantee secures a third person's debt, with three parties.
The creditor, the principal debtor and the surety. The surety's liability is co-extensive with the debtor's, so the creditor may sue the surety directly.
The surety is discharged by changes made without his consent, release of the debtor, giving the debtor time, or impairing his remedy.
On a default of 4.5 lakh, co-sureties share equally within their limits: Ravi pays his limit of 1 lakh, and the others pay 1.75 lakh each. || On a default of four lakh fifty thousand, co-sureties share equally within their limits. Ravi pays his limit of one lakh, and the others pay one lakh seventy five thousand each.
