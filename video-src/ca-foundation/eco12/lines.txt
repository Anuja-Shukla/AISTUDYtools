# Business Economics Lesson 1.2 — Basic Problems and the Price Mechanism
Welcome! This lesson is about the basic problems of an economy and the price mechanism.
Every economy must decide what to produce, how, for whom, and how to grow.
Rangpur's production possibilities show rising opportunity cost: each extra 10 bales of cloth costs more wheat, so the curve bows outward.
Points on the curve are efficient, inside show wasted resources, and outside are unattainable for now.
In a capitalist economy prices decide, in a socialist one planners decide, and a mixed economy like India's uses both.
Prices signal scarcity: a shortage raises the price, a surplus lowers it, guiding what is produced and who gets it.
