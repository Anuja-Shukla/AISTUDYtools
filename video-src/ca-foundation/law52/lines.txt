# Business Laws Lesson 5.2 — The Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881
Welcome! This lesson is about the Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881.
A promissory note is a promise to pay, a bill of exchange is an order to pay, and a cheque is an order on a banker payable on demand.
A holder in due course takes the instrument for value, before maturity and in good faith, and gets a title free from earlier defects.
A crossing tells the bank to pay only through a bank: general, special, account payee, or not negotiable.
Under section 138, present the cheque within three months, send notice within 30 days of learning of the dishonour, give the drawer 15 days to pay, and file within a month after that.
