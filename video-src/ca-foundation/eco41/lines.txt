# Business Economics Lesson 4.1 — Fiscal Functions and Centre-State Finance
Welcome! This lesson is about fiscal functions and centre-state finance.
Musgrave's three fiscal functions: allocation of resources, a fairer distribution of income, and stabilisation of the economy.
States spend more than they raise, a vertical imbalance, and differ from each other, a horizontal imbalance.
The Finance Commission, every five years, recommends the States' share of central taxes, how it is divided, and grants-in-aid.
GST rates are recommended by the GST Council, not the Finance Commission.
