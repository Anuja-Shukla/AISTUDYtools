# Business Economics Lesson 2.1 — Theory of Production
Welcome! This lesson is about the theory of production.
Land earns rent, labour wages, capital interest, and the entrepreneur profit.
With one oven, adding bakers raises output at first, then by less and less, and finally output falls.
Stage one runs to maximum average product, stage two to zero marginal product, and stage three has negative marginal product. Producers operate in stage two.
When all inputs rise together, returns to scale can be increasing, constant or decreasing.
