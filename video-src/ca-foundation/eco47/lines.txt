# Business Economics Lesson 4.7 — Monetary Policy
Welcome! This lesson is about monetary policy.
Since 2016, India targets CPI inflation of 4 percent, plus or minus 2 points. The six-member MPC sets the repo rate. || Since twenty sixteen, India has followed flexible inflation targeting: CPI inflation of four percent, plus or minus two percentage points. The six-member Monetary Policy Committee sets the repo rate.
The repo rate sits in a corridor, with the Standing Deposit Facility rate as the floor and the MSF rate as the ceiling.
Quantitative tools are rates, reserve ratios and open market operations; qualitative tools are selective credit controls.
If inflation reaches 6.8 percent, the MPC raises the repo rate; lending rates rise, demand cools, and inflation eases toward 4. || If inflation reaches six point eight percent, above the six percent limit, the MPC raises the repo rate. Lending rates rise, demand cools, and inflation eases back toward four percent.
Raising rates or ratios and selling securities tightens policy; the reverse eases it.
Try this: raise the repo rate. || Try this. To control inflation, what would the RBI most likely do? The answer is A, raise the repo rate.
