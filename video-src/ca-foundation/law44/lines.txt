# Business Laws Lesson 4.4 — The LLP Act, 2008
Welcome! This lesson is about the Limited Liability Partnership Act.
A limited liability partnership is a body corporate, a separate legal entity with perpetual succession and limited liability.
An LLP needs at least two partners, no maximum, and at least two designated partners who are individuals, one resident in India.
A partner is liable for his own wrongful act, but not for another partner's; the LLP itself is liable.
Unlike a firm, an LLP is a separate entity with limited liability and no maximum number of partners.
