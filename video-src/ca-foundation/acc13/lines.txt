# Accounting Lesson 1.3 — Capital and Revenue Items
Welcome! This lesson is about capital and revenue items.
Capital expenditure gives a lasting benefit, such as buying or improving an asset. Revenue expenditure keeps the business running this year.
A van, its installation-type costs and a shop extension are capital. Running costs like petrol, repairs and salaries are revenue.
Capital receipts include loans, fresh capital and sale of old assets. Revenue receipts are earned in normal business: sales, interest and rent.
Deferred revenue expenditure is revenue in nature but benefits several years, like a big ad campaign. Under current standards it is usually charged when incurred.
Getting it wrong changes profit. Treat a capital item as revenue and profit is understated; the reverse overstates it.
