# Business Economics Lesson 2.5 — Price-Output under Market Forms
Welcome! This lesson is about price and output under different market forms.
Every firm maximises profit where marginal revenue equals marginal cost, with marginal cost rising.
A competitive firm sets price equal to marginal cost, shuts down below minimum average variable cost, and earns normal profit in the long run.
With demand P equals 100 minus 2Q and marginal cost 20, the monopolist makes 20 units at 60 rupees, earning 600. || With demand P equals one hundred minus two Q, and marginal cost twenty, the monopolist makes twenty units at sixty rupees, and earns six hundred.
A discriminating monopolist charges more where demand is less elastic.
