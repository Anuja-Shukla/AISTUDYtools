# Business Laws Lesson 4.1 — General Nature of a Partnership
Welcome! This lesson is about the general nature of a partnership.
A partnership needs two or more persons, an agreement, a business, sharing of profits, and mutual agency.
Mutual agency is the true test: each partner is an agent of the others, and bound by what they do in the business.
Co-owners sharing rent, a Hindu undivided family business, a lender paid from profits, and an employee on profit-share are not partners.
A firm is not a separate legal person, may have up to 50 partners, and anyone who holds himself out as a partner is liable as one.
A minor can only be admitted to the benefits, and must elect within six months of majority; if silent, he becomes a partner.
