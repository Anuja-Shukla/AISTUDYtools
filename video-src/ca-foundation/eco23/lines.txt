# Business Economics Lesson 2.3 — Meaning and Types of Markets
Welcome! This lesson is about the meaning and types of markets.
Markets differ by the number of sellers, the kind of product and ease of entry.
A competitive firm faces a horizontal demand curve; other firms face a downward-sloping one, with marginal revenue below price.
In oligopoly, firms watch each other: prices are often rigid, explained by the kinked demand curve, or set by a leader or cartel.
