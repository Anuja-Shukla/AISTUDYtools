# Business Laws Lesson 3.1 — Formation of the Contract of Sale
Welcome! This lesson is about the formation of the contract of sale.
In a sale, ownership passes now; in an agreement to sell, it passes later or when a condition is met.
Goods means movable property, including shares and growing crops, but not money or actionable claims.
Goods may be existing, specific or unascertained, future, or contingent. A contract for future goods is always an agreement to sell.
Specific goods that had already perished make the contract void; if they perish later, before risk passes, an agreement to sell is avoided.
Neha agrees on 10 May to buy a specific fridge destroyed by fire on 9 May.
