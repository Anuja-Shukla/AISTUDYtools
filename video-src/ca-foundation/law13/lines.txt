# Business Laws Lesson 1.3 — Consideration
Welcome! This lesson is about consideration.
Under section 2(d), consideration is an act, abstinence or promise, done at the desire of the promisor, by the promisee or any other person.
It must move at the promisor's desire, may come from anyone, need not be adequate, but must be real and lawful.
In India a stranger to consideration can sue, but a stranger to the contract generally cannot.
Section 25 saves three agreements without consideration: love and affection in a registered writing, compensating past voluntary service, and a written promise to pay a time-barred debt.
Dev repaired the cafe's pipe at the owner's request, and the owner later promised 3,000.
