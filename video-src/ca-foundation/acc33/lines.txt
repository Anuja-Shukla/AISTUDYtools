# Accounting Lesson 3.3 — Accounts of Not-for-Profit Organisations
Welcome! This lesson is about accounts of not-for-profit organisations.
A club prepares a receipts and payments account, a summary of cash, and an income and expenditure account, which works like a profit and loss account.
An excess of income is a surplus; an excess of expenditure is a deficit. It goes to the capital fund.
Green Valley Sports Club received 2.4 lakh. Adjusting for outstanding and advance subscriptions gives income of 2,36,000. || Green Valley Sports Club received two lakh forty thousand in subscriptions. Adjusting for outstanding and advance amounts gives income of two lakh thirty six thousand.
Entrance fees, legacies, specific donations and life membership fees each need careful treatment, often as capital receipts.
