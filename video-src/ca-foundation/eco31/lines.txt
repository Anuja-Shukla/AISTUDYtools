# Business Economics Lesson 3.1 — National Income Accounting
Welcome! This lesson is about national income accounting.
Income flows between households and firms. Saving, taxes and imports leak out; investment, government spending and exports are injected.
Add net factor income from abroad to go from domestic to national; subtract depreciation for net; subtract net indirect taxes for factor cost.
From spending, GDP is 9,300 crore. Adjusting for factor income, depreciation and net indirect taxes gives national income of 7,800 crore. || From spending, GDP is nine thousand three hundred crore. Adjusting for factor income, depreciation and net indirect taxes gives national income of seven thousand eight hundred crore.
National income can be measured by value added, by incomes, or by expenditure.
Transfers, second-hand sales, financial transactions and household work are not included.
