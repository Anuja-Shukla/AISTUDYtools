# Business Economics Lesson 2.2 — Theory of Cost
Welcome! This lesson is about the theory of cost.
Explicit costs are paid to others; implicit costs are the value of the owner's own resources. Economic profit deducts both.
With fixed cost of 600, average fixed cost keeps falling, while average variable and total costs fall and then rise.
Average fixed cost always falls, average variable and total cost are U-shaped, and marginal cost cuts both at their lowest points.
In the long run, economies of scale lower average cost, until diseconomies push it up again.
