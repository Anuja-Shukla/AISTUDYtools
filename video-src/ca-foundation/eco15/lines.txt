# Business Economics Lesson 1.5 — Supply
Welcome! This lesson is about supply.
When price rises, quantity supplied rises, because production becomes more profitable.
Supply shifts with input prices, technology, taxes and subsidies, prices of related goods, and the number of firms.
When the price of bread rises from 40 to 50, supply rises from 300 to 420 loaves: elasticity of supply is 1.6. || When the price of bread rises from forty to fifty, supply rises from three hundred to four hundred twenty loaves. Elasticity of supply is one point six.
A straight supply line through the origin has elasticity one; one meeting the price axis is elastic; one meeting the quantity axis is inelastic.
Supply is more elastic the more time producers have to adjust.
