# Accounting Lesson 2.4 — Subsidiary Books
Welcome! This lesson is about subsidiary books.
Subsidiary books split the journal by type of transaction, dividing the work and saving postings.
Credit purchases and sales of goods go to the purchases and sales books, returns to the returns books, cash and bank to the cash book, and the rest to the journal proper.
Only goods for resale go to the purchases book. A computer bought on credit for office use goes to the journal proper.
Individual suppliers and customers are posted one by one; the book's total goes to the purchases or sales account at the end of the period.
The journal proper handles opening and closing entries, adjustments, rectifications, credit dealings in assets, and transfers.
