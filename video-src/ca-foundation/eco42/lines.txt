# Business Economics Lesson 4.2 — Market Failure and Government Intervention
Welcome! This lesson is about market failure and government intervention.
Markets fail through market power, externalities, public goods, and asymmetric information.
A negative externality like pollution leads to over-production; a positive one like vaccination leads to under-production.
A pure public good is non-rival and non-excludable, so free riders leave it under-supplied by the market.
Adverse selection happens before a deal; moral hazard happens after it.
Each tonne costs the firm 8,000 but causes 2,000 of damage. A Pigovian tax of 2,000 makes it face the full social cost. || Each tonne costs the firm eight thousand rupees, but causes two thousand rupees of pollution damage. A Pigovian tax of two thousand rupees a tonne makes the firm face the full social cost.
