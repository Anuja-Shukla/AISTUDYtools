# Lesson 3.5 — Theoretical Distributions
Welcome! This lesson covers three theoretical distributions: the binomial, the Poisson and the normal.
These models describe how a random outcome behaves. The binomial and Poisson count things, so they are discrete. The normal is continuous: it describes measurements.
Use the binomial when there is a fixed number of trials, n, each with two outcomes, success or failure, the same success probability p every time, and independent trials.
