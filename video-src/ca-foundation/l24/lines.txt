# Lesson 2.4 — Blood Relations
Welcome! This lesson is about blood relations: generations, family names, family trees and coded relations.
Blood relation questions test two things at every step: the generation of each person, and their gender.
Think in generations. Grandparents are two levels up, parents one up, you and your siblings are level, your children one down, and grandchildren two down.
Learn the key names. A parent's brother or sister is your uncle or aunt. Their child is your cousin. Your sibling's son or daughter is your nephew or niece.
And the in-laws. Your spouse's parents are your father- and mother-in-law. Your spouse's siblings, and your siblings' spouses, are brothers- and sisters-in-law. Your child's spouse is a son- or daughter-in-law.
Pointing to a man, Riya says: “He is the son of my grandfather's only son.” How is the man related to Riya? || Pointing to a man, Riya says: he is the son of my grandfather's only son. How is the man related to Riya?
Work from the speaker outward. Riya's grandfather's only son must be Riya's father. The son of her father is her brother. || Work from the speaker outward. Riya's grandfather's only son must be Riya's own father. And the son of her father is her brother.
For longer puzzles, draw a family tree: a square or + for males, a circle or − for females, a double line for a married couple, and a vertical line down to their children. Keep each generation on its own level. || For longer puzzles, draw a family tree. Use a square or a plus sign for males, and a circle or a minus sign for females. A double line joins a married couple, and a vertical line goes down to their children. Keep each generation on its own level.
Never assume a gender that is not stated. “A is the child of B” does not tell you whether B is the father or the mother. || Never assume a gender that is not stated. A is the child of B does not tell you whether B is the father or the mother.
Coded relations define symbols. Suppose P × Q means P is the brother of Q, and P + Q means P is the mother of Q. What does A × B + C mean? || Coded relations define symbols. Suppose P times Q means P is the brother of Q, and P plus Q means P is the mother of Q. What does A times B plus C mean?
Decode one symbol at a time. A is the brother of B, and B is the mother of C. So A is C's mother's brother: C's maternal uncle. || Decode one symbol at a time. A is the brother of B, and B is the mother of C. So A is C's mother's brother. That makes A the maternal uncle of C.
Try this: A is B's sister, C is B's mother, and D is C's father. How is A related to D? A is a child of C, and C is a child of D, so A is D's granddaughter. || Try this. A is B's sister. C is B's mother. And D is C's father. How is A related to D? A is a child of C, and C is a child of D. So A is D's granddaughter. The answer is A.
