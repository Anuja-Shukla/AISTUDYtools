# Business Laws Lesson 4.2 — Relations of Partners
Welcome! This lesson is about the relations of partners.
Partners owe utmost good faith, true accounts, indemnity for fraud, and must not make secret profits or compete.
If the deed is silent: equal profits, no salary, no interest on capital, and 6 percent on advances.
Without express authority, a partner cannot submit to arbitration, open a bank account in his own name, compromise a claim, withdraw a suit, admit liability, deal in immovable property, or enter a partnership for the firm.
All partners are jointly and severally liable; a retiring partner stays liable until public notice is given.
