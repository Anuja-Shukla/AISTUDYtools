# Business Economics Lesson 5.3 — Trade Negotiations
Welcome! This lesson is about trade negotiations.
Integration runs from preferential deals to free trade areas, customs unions, common markets and economic unions.
GATT began in 1947. The Uruguay Round created the World Trade Organization on the first of January 1995.
Most-favoured-nation treatment means no discrimination between members; national treatment means imports are treated like domestic goods.
Major agreements cover agriculture, services, intellectual property and dispute settlement.
The Doha Round, launched in 2001, has made slow progress, with deals on trade facilitation and fisheries subsidies.
