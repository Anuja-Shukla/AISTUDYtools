# Business Laws Lesson 2.5 — Bailment and Pledge
Welcome! This lesson is about bailment and pledge.
Bailment is delivery of goods for a purpose, with the same goods to be returned.
A bailee must take the care a man of ordinary prudence takes of his own goods; if he does, he is not liable for loss.
A particular lien covers work done on the goods; a general lien, for bankers, factors, wharfingers, attorneys and policy brokers, covers the whole balance.
If the pawnor defaults, the pawnee may sue, or sell after reasonable notice; any shortfall is still due and any surplus returned.
