# Business Economics Lesson 2.4 — Determination of Prices
Welcome! This lesson is about the determination of prices.
Setting demand equal to supply gives an equilibrium price of 20 rupees and quantity of 800 kg. || Setting demand equal to supply gives an equilibrium price of twenty rupees and a quantity of eight hundred kilos.
Demand up raises price and quantity; supply up lowers price and raises quantity; when both shift, one result is uncertain.
A ceiling of 15 causes a shortage of 250 kg; a floor of 25 causes a surplus of 250 kg. || A price ceiling of fifteen causes a shortage of two hundred fifty kilos. A floor of twenty five causes a surplus of two hundred fifty kilos.
Try this: price rises. || Try this. If demand rises and supply falls, what happens to price? The answer is A. It rises.
