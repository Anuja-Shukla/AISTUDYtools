# Business Laws Lesson 2.6 — Agency
Welcome! This lesson is about agency.
A principal authorises an agent, who makes contracts that bind the principal to third parties. No consideration is needed.
Agency can be express, implied, by estoppel, of necessity, or by ratification.
Ratification is approval later, with full knowledge. It relates back to the act, but cannot harm a third party.
An agent is usually not personally liable, except for a foreign or undisclosed principal, or by agreement.
Agency ends on revocation, renunciation, death, insanity or insolvency, but an agency coupled with interest is irrevocable.
