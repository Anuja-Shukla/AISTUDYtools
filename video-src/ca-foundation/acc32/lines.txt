# Accounting Lesson 3.2 — Manufacturing Accounts
Welcome! This lesson is about manufacturing accounts.
Materials consumed plus direct wages and expenses give prime cost; add factory overheads, adjust for work-in-progress and scrap, and you get the cost of production.
Materials consumed: opening 30,000 plus purchases 2 lakh minus closing 40,000, equals 1,90,000. || Materials consumed are opening stock of thirty thousand, plus purchases of two lakh, minus closing stock of forty thousand. That is one lakh ninety thousand.
Prime cost is 2,80,000. Adding overheads and adjusting for work-in-progress and scrap gives a cost of production of 3,30,000. || Prime cost is two lakh eighty thousand. Adding overheads, and adjusting for work-in-progress and scrap, gives a cost of production of three lakh thirty thousand.
The cost of production moves to the trading account. With finished goods adjusted, cost of goods sold is 3.2 lakh and gross profit is 1,30,000. || The cost of production moves to the trading account. After adjusting finished goods, cost of goods sold is three lakh twenty thousand, and gross profit is one lakh thirty thousand.
Try this: prime cost is materials consumed plus direct wages plus direct expenses. || Try this. What is prime cost? The answer is A. Materials consumed, plus direct wages, plus direct expenses.
