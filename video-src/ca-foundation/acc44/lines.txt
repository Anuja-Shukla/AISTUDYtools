# Accounting Lesson 4.4 — Retirement of a Partner
Welcome! This lesson is about the retirement of a partner.
When Bina retires and the others share 3 to 2, Arun gains a tenth and Chetan two-tenths: a gaining ratio of 1 to 2. || When Bina retires and the others share three to two, Arun gains one tenth and Chetan two tenths. The gaining ratio is one to two.
Bina's share of goodwill, 36,000, is charged to the gaining partners: 12,000 and 24,000. || Bina's share of goodwill, thirty six thousand, is charged to the gaining partners. Twelve thousand and twenty four thousand.
The retiring partner gets capital plus shares of goodwill, reserves, revaluation and profit to date, minus drawings.
The amount is paid at once or transferred to a loan account and paid in instalments with interest.
Try this: gaining ratio is new share minus old share. || Try this. What is the gaining ratio? The answer is A, new share minus old share.
