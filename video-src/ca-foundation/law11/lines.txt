# Business Laws Lesson 1.1 — The Indian Regulatory Framework
Welcome! This lesson is about the Indian regulatory framework.
Indian law comes from the Constitution, Acts of Parliament and State Legislatures, rules made under them, judicial precedent, and custom.
A Bill is passed by both Houses of Parliament and becomes an Act on the President's assent; the details are then filled in by rules.
Each regulator has its own field: RBI for banks, SEBI for securities markets, MCA for companies and LLPs, IRDAI for insurance, ICAI for chartered accountants, and PFRDA for pensions.
The Supreme Court's decisions bind all courts in India. Tribunals like the NCLT and NCLAT handle company and insolvency matters.
