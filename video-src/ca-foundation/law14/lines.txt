# Business Laws Lesson 1.4 — Other Essential Elements of a Contract
Welcome! This lesson is about the other essential elements of a contract.
A person is competent if a major, of sound mind and not disqualified. A minor's agreement is void from the beginning.
Coercion, undue influence, fraud and misrepresentation make a contract voidable. A mistake of fact by both parties makes it void.
Fraud is deliberate and allows rescission and damages; misrepresentation is innocent and allows rescission.
Agreements in restraint of marriage, trade or legal proceedings, uncertain agreements and wagers are expressly void.
