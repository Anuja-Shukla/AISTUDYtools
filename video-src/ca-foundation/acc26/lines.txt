# Accounting Lesson 2.6 — Rectification of Errors
Welcome! This lesson is about rectification of errors.
Errors are of omission, commission, principle, or compensating errors that cancel each other.
Furniture of 8,000 wrongly debited to purchases is corrected by debiting furniture and crediting purchases. || Furniture worth eight thousand was wrongly debited to purchases. Correct it by debiting furniture, and crediting purchases.
To rectify, ask what was done, what should have been done, and pass the entry that moves from one to the other.
If an error affects only one side, the other side of the correcting entry goes to suspense. For example, sales overcast by 1,000: debit sales, credit suspense. || If an error affects only one side, the other side of the correcting entry goes to suspense. For example, if sales were overcast by one thousand, debit sales and credit suspense.
Errors found after closing the books affect nominal accounts through a profit and loss adjustment account.
Try this: debit furniture, credit purchases, for 8,000. || Try this. Furniture was debited to purchases. What is the rectifying entry? The answer is A. Debit furniture, and credit purchases.
