# Business Economics Lesson 5.5 — International Capital Movements
Welcome! This lesson is about international capital movements.
Foreign capital comes as direct investment, portfolio investment, borrowing, aid and NRI deposits.
FDI brings a lasting interest and control, ten percent or more of a listed company in India. FPI is a financial stake without control.
A new plant is greenfield FDI, buying a local firm is brownfield, a partner is a joint venture, and a five percent stake is FPI.
FDI brings capital, technology and jobs, but also profit outflows and transfer pricing risks.
India allows most FDI through the automatic route; some sectors need approval, and a few are prohibited.
