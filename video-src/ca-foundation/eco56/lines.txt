# Business Economics Lesson 5.6 — Indian Economy
Welcome! This lesson is about the Indian economy.
At independence, India was a poor, agrarian economy with weak industry. It chose a mixed economy with a leading public sector.
Twelve Five-Year Plans ran from 1951 to 2017, with the Green Revolution and bank nationalisation, but growth was slow.
The 1991 crisis led to liberalisation, privatisation and globalisation.
Later reforms include the FRBM Act, NITI Aayog, the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, inflation targeting and GST.
Today services give the largest share of output, while agriculture still employs the most workers.
