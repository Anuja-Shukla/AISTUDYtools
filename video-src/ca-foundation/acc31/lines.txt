# Accounting Lesson 3.1 — Final Accounts of Sole Proprietors
Welcome! This lesson is about final accounts of sole proprietors.
Final accounts start from the trial balance: a trading account for gross profit, a profit and loss account for net profit, and a balance sheet for the financial position.
Net sales of 4.9 lakh plus closing stock, minus opening stock, net purchases and direct expenses, gives a gross profit of 1,75,000. || Net sales of four lakh ninety thousand plus closing stock, minus opening stock, net purchases and direct expenses, gives a gross profit of one lakh seventy five thousand.
Each adjustment appears twice: once in the profit and loss account, and once in the balance sheet.
Gross profit plus other income, minus the indirect expenses after adjustments, gives a net profit of 85,000. || Gross profit plus other income, minus the indirect expenses after adjustments, gives a net profit of eighty five thousand.
The gross profit ratio is about 35.7 percent and the net profit ratio about 17.3 percent of net sales. || The gross profit ratio is about thirty five point seven percent, and the net profit ratio about seventeen point three percent of net sales.
