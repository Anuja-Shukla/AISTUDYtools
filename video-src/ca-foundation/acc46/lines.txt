# Accounting Lesson 4.6 — Dissolution of a Partnership Firm
Welcome! This lesson is about the dissolution of a partnership firm.
The realisation account takes assets at book value on the debit side and liabilities on the credit side, then records what is actually realised and paid.
Assets of 1.5 lakh realise 1.3 lakh, creditors of 40,000 are settled for 38,000, and expenses are 2,000. || Assets of one lakh fifty thousand realise one lakh thirty thousand. Creditors of forty thousand are settled for thirty eight thousand. Expenses are two thousand.
Debits total 1.9 lakh and credits 1.7 lakh: a loss on realisation of 20,000, shared 12,000 and 8,000. || Debits total one lakh ninety thousand and credits one lakh seventy thousand. That is a loss on realisation of twenty thousand, shared twelve thousand and eight thousand.
Assets go first to outside creditors, then partners' loans, then capitals, and any surplus in the profit ratio.
Try this: assets go to the realisation account at book value. || Try this. At what value are assets transferred to the realisation account? The answer is A, book value.
