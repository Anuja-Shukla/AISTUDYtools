# Business Laws Lesson 4.3 — Registration and Dissolution of a Firm
Welcome! This lesson is about registration and dissolution of a firm.
Registration is optional, but an unregistered firm loses important rights.
An unregistered firm cannot sue third parties, and partners cannot sue it, but third parties can sue it, and a suit for dissolution is allowed.
A firm may be dissolved by agreement, compulsorily, on contingencies, by notice, or by the court.
On dissolution, assets pay outside debts first, then partners' advances, then capital, and any surplus in the profit ratio.
