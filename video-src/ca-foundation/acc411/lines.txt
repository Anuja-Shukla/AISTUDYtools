# Accounting Lesson 4.11 — Redemption of Preference Shares
Welcome! This lesson is about the redemption of preference shares.
Preference shares may be redeemed only when fully paid, out of profits or a fresh issue of shares. Profits used must be transferred to a capital redemption reserve.
Sunrise redeems 10 lakh of preference shares. A fresh issue covers 4 lakh, so 6 lakh comes from profits and goes to capital redemption reserve. || Sunrise redeems ten lakh of preference shares. A fresh issue covers four lakh, so six lakh comes from profits, and goes to capital redemption reserve.
The 50,000 premium on redemption is met from securities premium or profits. || The fifty thousand premium on redemption is met from securities premium, or from profits.
Only the nominal value of new shares counts towards redemption; debenture money never does.
Try this: profits used for redemption go to the capital redemption reserve. || Try this. Preference shares redeemed out of profits need a transfer to which reserve? The answer is A, the capital redemption reserve.
