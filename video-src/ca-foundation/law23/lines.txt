# Business Laws Lesson 2.3 — Contingent and Quasi Contracts
Welcome! This lesson is about contingent and quasi contracts.
A contingent contract depends on an uncertain event collateral to the contract, as in insurance.
If the event must happen, enforce when it does; if it must not happen, enforce when it becomes impossible; time limits and impossible events follow sections 35 and 36.
Quasi contracts stop unjust enrichment: necessaries, an interested payer, non-gratuitous acts, the finder of goods, and money paid by mistake.
A contingent contract is valid; a wager is void.
